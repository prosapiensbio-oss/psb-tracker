Hello,

I'm Filip Stráňavský, a certified FP practitioner based in Brno, Czech
Republic (ProSapiens Biomechanic). I have two questions about policy scope
where I'd rather ask than assume.

**1 — Does the client NDA requirement cover dental professionals?**

The policy lists trainers, physiotherapists, manual therapists and "any other
health focused professional" as clients who should sign the NDA before
training. I have a dentist and a dental hygienist among my clients.

Reading the list, every named profession works with movement or manual
therapy — so the intent may be "anyone who could apply the method with their
own clients" rather than "anyone with a medical education". A dentist has the
education but no professional route to applying FP. I'd like to know which
reading is correct, because it decides whether I ask them to sign.

**2 — May the verification link also go on my own website?**

The Handbook specifies the verification link for the website field of my
Instagram profile, and provides the FP-hosted page for websites. My site has
a page explaining Functional Patterns which is, by a wide margin, my
most-visited subpage — the people reading it are exactly the ones checking
whether my certification is real.

I couldn't find the placement either permitted or prohibited for a
practitioner's own site, so I'd rather have it in writing than infer it.

Thank you for your time.

**3 — Reaction videos to public figures' training content**

I'd like to post short reaction videos on my studio's business account
(not my FP MIP): a few seconds of a publicly available clip — for example
a celebrity's training routine or a viral mobility drill — followed by my
own commentary on the general biomechanics of what is shown.

My reading of the Practitioners Policy is that this is fine as long as I
(a) don't explain or demonstrate FP techniques, (b) don't use FP branding
or speak on FP's behalf, (c) keep the tone about the movement, not the
person, and (d) don't post it on the MIP, since the clip itself contradicts
FP methodology. Is that reading correct, and is there anything you'd want
me to avoid — or would you prefer this format on the MIP with FP credited?
